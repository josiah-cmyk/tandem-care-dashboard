      Metric definitions and rationale

      Metrics follow the University of Washington Advancing Integrated Mental Health Solutions (AIMS) Center Collaborative Care measures, Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) requirements for the Collaborative Care Model, and the Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set (HEDIS) behavioral health measures that count toward Medicare Advantage Star Ratings.
